Buying guide

Buy an IPTV playlist: what you get, what it costs and how to buy safely

An IPTV playlist is just a login or a link — so what you are really buying is the service behind it. Here is how to tell a good one from a bad one before you pay.

2026-10-12•4 min read•IPTV Playlist team

The quick answer

When you buy an IPTV playlist you receive an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login (server, username, password) that you paste into a player app. Pay for a short term first, check that support answers before you pay, pay by a traceable method, and install the player app only from your device's official store.

People search "buy IPTV playlist" expecting to download a file. In practice you are buying access: a playlist that points to streams, plus the support that keeps it working. This guide explains what arrives after you pay, what a fair price looks like, and how to avoid the sellers that disappear a month later.

What you actually receive

An IPTV playlist comes in one of three formats. Most providers send all of them so you can pick whichever your app prefers:

  • M3U link — a single URL that returns a text list of channels. Every player can read it. Our guide to M3U, EPG and portals explains the format.
  • Xtream Codes login — a server address, username and password. Apps like IPTV Smarters or TiviMate use it to load channels, the TV guide and on-demand sections separately, which is faster and tidier than one big M3U file.
  • MAG / portal — a portal URL tied to your device's MAC address, used by MAG boxes and some Smart TV apps.

You also need an EPG (electronic programme guide) link if the player does not load it automatically. If the basics are new, start with what an IPTV playlist is.

What a fair price looks like

Playlist subscriptions are sold by term, not by channel. Longer terms cost less per month, but they also lock in more money if the service turns out to be poor. A sensible approach is to start short, test properly, then extend.

Six checks before you buy

  1. Ask a technical question first. "Which format works best on my Fire TV?" A useful answer within minutes tells you support exists.
  2. Check the number of connections. One connection means one screen streaming at a time.
  3. Confirm your device. Smart TV, Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV, phone or computer — see supported devices.
  4. Get the app from an official store. The US Federal Trade Commission warns that pirate streaming apps frequently carry malware. A playlist never requires a sideloaded APK.
  5. Check your connection. Roughly 8–15 Mbit/s per HD stream and 25 Mbit/s or more for 4K. A cable to the TV fixes most buffering.
  6. Read the legal framing. A serious provider tells you what it does and does not grant rights to. Our article on whether IPTV is legal explains why that matters.

Red flags that mean "don't buy"

  • No price until you message, or a price that changes in chat.
  • "Install this APK" instead of an app from an official store.

  • Claims of 100% uptime or every channel in the world.

Frequently asked questions

What do I get when I buy an IPTV playlist?

Usually an M3U link, an Xtream Codes login (server, username and password) or a MAG portal address, plus an EPG link for the TV guide. You paste these into a compatible player app on your device.

Is M3U or Xtream Codes better?

Xtream Codes is usually faster and better organised because the app loads live channels, the guide and on-demand sections separately. M3U works in almost every player and is the universal fallback.

How much does an IPTV playlist cost?

Prices are set by term. With IPTV Playlist, plans run from €39.99 for 12 months to €84.99 for 24 months, as a one-time payment with no automatic renewal.

Can I use one playlist on several devices?

You can install it on several devices, but the number that can stream at the same time is limited by your connections. Extra connections are 15% cheaper each.

Do I need a special app?

You need a compatible IPTV player from your device's official app store, such as one that accepts Xtream Codes or M3U links. No sideloaded APK is required.
