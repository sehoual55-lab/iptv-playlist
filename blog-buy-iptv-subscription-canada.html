Buying an IPTV subscription in Canada: what to know first

Canada's courts now let broadcasters block pirate IPTV streams in real time. Here is what that means for you, and how to buy safely.

2026-10-12•4 min read•IPTV Playlist team

The quick answer

Licensed streaming in Canada includes Crave, Sportsnet+, TSN+ and free CBC Gem. Since 2022, Canada's Federal Court has granted "dynamic" blocking orders that let internet providers block pirate IPTV streams of live sports as they happen. If you buy an IPTV service, choose one that is transparent about what it provides, and test it before paying for a long term.

Search "buy IPTV subscription Canada" and you will find services promising every NHL game for a few dollars. Before you pay, it helps to understand the licensed options, what Canadian courts have done about pirate streams, and the checks that protect your money.

Canada's dynamic blocking orders

In 2022, Rogers, Bell, TSN and Groupe TVA won Canada's first dynamic blocking order from the Federal Court. It requires internet providers to block the servers carrying pirate NHL streams during live games — and to add new servers as they appear. Similar orders have followed for other sports. In practice, unlicensed sports streams in Canada are increasingly likely to go dark exactly when you want to watch.

What to check before you buy in Canada

  1. What is being sold? A clear provider says whether it sells content rights or a technical service. See why licensing decides legality — the principle is the same in Canada.
  2. Test during a live game. That is when blocking and overloaded servers show up.
  3. Price and currency. Confirm the total and the currency before paying. Card payments convert automatically.
  4. Official apps only. Avoid sideloaded APKs and pre-loaded boxes; they are a common source of malware.
  5. Your connection. Around 8–15 Mbit/s per HD stream, 25 Mbit/s or more for 4K, multiplied by the screens watching at once.

Frequently asked questions

Is IPTV legal in Canada?

IPTV technology is legal, and services such as Crave, Sportsnet+ and CBC Gem use it. Streaming channels without the rights holders' permission is not, and Canadian courts have ordered internet providers to block pirate streams.

What is a dynamic blocking order?

A court order, first granted in Canada in 2022, that requires internet providers to block servers carrying pirate live-sports streams during games and to add new servers as they appear.

What is the cheapest legal way to stream TV in Canada?

CBC Gem is free. Crave, Sportsnet+ and TSN+ are paid subscriptions that can be bought separately without a full TV package.

Can I pay for IPTV Playlist in Canadian dollars?

Prices are listed in euros. Card payments are converted to Canadian dollars automatically by your bank.

Which devices work in Canada?

Any device with a compatible player app from its official store: Smart TVs, Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV, phones, tablets and computers.
